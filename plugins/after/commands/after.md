---
description: Queue prompts to run after the current turn or task settles, without interrupting or steering it
---

Queue management command for the `/after` plugin:
- `/after <task>`: Append `<task>` to the queue.
- `/before <task>`: Prepend `<task>` to the front of the queue.
- `/after`: Show all tasks currently in queue.
- `/after clear`: Clear all queued tasks.
- `/after run`: Trigger the next queued task immediately if idle.
